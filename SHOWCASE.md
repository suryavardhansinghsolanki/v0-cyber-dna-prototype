```
                    ↓
```

### 2. Behavioral Profiling
```
Cyber-DNA learns YOUR unique patterns:

  • New York (60% of logins)
  • Los Angeles (25% of logins)
  • Washington DC (15% of logins)

  • iPhone: 45%
  • MacBook: 35%
  • iPad: 20%

  • Average: $500 per transaction
```

### 3. Smart Alert System
```
4 Severity Levels:

🔴 CRITICAL (Score > 0.8)
   Action: IMMEDIATE block/lock
   Example: Login from new country, large fraud

🟠 HIGH (Score 0.6-0.8)
   Action: Verify identity required
   Example: New device, phishing link

🟡 MEDIUM (Score 0.4-0.6)
   Action: Monitor closely
   Example: Unusual time, unusual amount

🟢 LOW (Score < 0.4)
   Action: Log and learn
   Example: Minor deviation from baseline
```
